Goods-In Check Sample data

Every part on the bench, held to its PO and its paperwork.

Notes

  1. The release document must be in the box and of a kind the PO line accepts.
  2. Part number exactly as ordered, dash number included, or an approved alternate written on the PO line.
  3. Quantity counted, quantity on the document and quantity ordered agree.
  4. Status/Work satisfies the condition ordered. Overhauled needs Overhauled. Serviceable takes Overhauled, Repaired or Inspected/Tested. This is the shop's own rule.
  5. Serial on the data plate matches the document. Shelf life left at receipt meets the PO minimum.
  6. A tracking number already on an earlier receipt is not accepted twice.
  7. Anything that cannot be read waits for the inspector. Nothing is guessed, and a person releases every line.
